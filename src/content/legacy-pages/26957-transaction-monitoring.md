## Transaction Monitoring

Harnessing big data is one thing, making it work for you is another.  Our team helps the retail loss prevention and asset protection community turn their data into relevant story lines that foster decision making processes throughout their organization.  This leads to improved and sustainable performance in areas such as shrink, profitability, sales, service, and labor.

A fully leveraged Transaction Monitoring program can drive a **10-15%** reduction in annual shrink losses.
