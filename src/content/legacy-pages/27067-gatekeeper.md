## Gatekeeper with the Stores Consulting Group

Communications are often inconsistent and come in varying forms. *t*SCG provides gatekeeper and task management solutions to manage the flow of communications to stores.  Gatekeepers decide which tasks corporate sends to stores, what are the priorities, and at what times – thereby shielding stores from being inundated with constant, unnecessary communications.

#### Implement

During implementation, *t*SCG works with throughout the organization to focus on change management and compliance.  This ensures that stores are getting the right information at the right time.

-   Create task manager protocols
-   Work with contributors
-   Establish communication requirements

*t*SCG works together with the client to ensure a continuation and sustainability plan are in place.  *t*SCG helps the organization transition from “project mode” to the way the client does business.

-   Create business calendar of activities
-   Continual feedback to contributors
