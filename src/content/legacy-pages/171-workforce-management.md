***Workforce Management – Consulting Partnerships***

-   Support product implementation process
-   Develop and maintain knowledge transfer mechanisms to ensure chain wide understanding and compliance

***Workforce Management – Planning for the Future***

-   Outside of the box thinking to better schedule for today’s associates
-   Employer of choice scheduling opportunities
-   Multiple schedules created at once – well in advance of the workweek based on compliance to local ordinances
-   Centralization and automation of schedules / Digital Schedules
-   No pre-scheduling full schedules – Shift marketplaces where open shifts can be picked up by willing employees – open shifts allowing for lifestyle balance scheduling
-   Associate BYOD access, shift selection and swapping
-   Social media with communication and engagement of the employee in understanding of the business to ensure alignment and better customer engagement
-   Workforce orchestration for learning management, WFM, HCM, and IOT items
-   Gig pay options – Express Pay, Gig workers
