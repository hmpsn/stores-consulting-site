## Shrink and Profit Recovery

*t*SCG takes a holistic approach to shrink and profit recovery focusing on sales growth, non-customer facing waste, and all areas of loss. We work with our clients to develop a strategic roadmap with short term tactical initiatives and a long term plan to achieve their goals.

Our clients typically achieve an **85-120 basis point improvement** in overall shrink, this does not include projected increases to net margin improvements by sales growth.
