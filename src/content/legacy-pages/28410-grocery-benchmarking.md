## Grocery Benchmarking



#### tSCG Benchmarks

-   Contain over twenty traditional food and supercenter retailers
-   Most retailer performance data is from projects in the past four years
-   We use actual performance data and not published numbers
-   We discard extreme performance indicators and perceived bad numbers
-   We minimize impact of the differences for regions, union, inflation and recession trends in wage, item price and inventory cost
-   All retailers in database report shrink at cost to retail sales and calculate inventory value at an average cost, however allow for some differences in retail to cost accounting and differences in how retail counts are converted to cost
-   We do not disclose specific client metric
