## Supply Chain

Integrating supply chain to support the overall goals of the organization is key to driving customer satisfaction and sales. Retailers have transitioned from “optimizing the function” to understanding functional roles in achieving customer loyalty and highest service levels at an acceptable cost.

[;](/)

***Logistics, warehouse operations, supplier collaboration and end to end measurement are critical to achieve a world class supply chain capability. tSCG can help you understand your supply chain opportunities and drive cost-effective changes that will help you achieve your supply chain goals.***

-   Warehouse labor cost reductions 10%-15%
-   Fuel savings of 20%+
-   Service levels achieving 99%+
-   Shelf in stocks of 98.5%+
