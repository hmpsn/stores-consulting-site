## Digital Consumer Profitability

70% of Americans shop online regularly.  57% have picked up an online order in a store. With these percentages projected to increase in years to come, is your operation ready to provide a high level of service to your customer through every channel?  *t*SCG can help

[;](/)

Consumers expect to receive the same level of service regardless of which way they choose to shop.  *t*SCG can help align your organization’s strategy to support the multi-channel consumer and maintain profitability.



#### Online Sales

-   **Category Management:** What products to sell online
-   **Operations:** Where, when and how to fulfill online orders
-   **Shipping:** How to route orders, zone skipping, selecting carrier(s)
-   **Inventory Management:** How to align the P&L and designate ownership of inventory



#### Buy Online - Pickup in Store

-   **Category Management:** What is available for in-store pickup
-   **Strategy:** How to maximize the customer’s in-store experience
-   **Service Level Comitments:** How to stay competitive and exceed customer expectations
-   **Operations**: What is the best in-store process to support service levels
-   **Scheduling and Forecasting:** How to maintain the proper staffing levels to support the business
-   **Inventory Management:**  How to ensure in-store inventory accuracy is maintained physically and systematically



#### In-store Support

-   **Strategy:** How can/should your store teams leverage online services to increase size of basket
-   **Strategy:** How to align the P&L to promote combined sales goals



#### Reverse Logistics

-   **Strategy:** How to handle online only items, or SKU’s that are not assigned to return store
-   **Operations:** How can returns be processed efficiently
